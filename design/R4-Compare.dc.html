Exposure A [$X.XT] on [date] · [N] companies

Circle area = total value of the chosen companies, same scale for both. Values are US dollars, not adjusted for inflation, mostly year-end records, so "lowest" and "highest" mean recorded. Animation: on any change the circles grow or shrink into place; with reduced motion they switch at once. Numbers in [brackets] are placeholders.
