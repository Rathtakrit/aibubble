---
title: "Debt and the gap"
date: 2026-10-08
players: [openai, oracle]
---
Building costs money now. The revenue is supposed to come later. In 2026 the distance between the two becomes the story.

OpenAI's accounts for 2025 show $13.07 billion of revenue against a $20.9 billion operating loss. Oracle, which borrowed heavily to build for OpenAI, sees its credit rating cut to one step above junk, the grade where lenders start to doubt they will be repaid.

On 8 October 2026 OpenAI's yearly revenue is reported to be running near $50 billion, about $20 billion below what investors had been told.
