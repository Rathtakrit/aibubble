The build-out leans on borrowed money. On 5 October 2026 the Kansas City Fed reports that AI firms' share of sales of investment-grade bonds, the safer kind of loan split into tradeable pieces, rises from 2% in 2023 to 10% in 2026. The separate companies set up to build data centres finance 90% of their assets with debt, money borrowed and paid back with interest.

Oracle owes $82.2 billion in long-term debt and pays $923 million of interest a quarter. On 21 October 2025 Meta moves its $27 billion Hyperion data centre into a joint venture, a company owned by two or more firms, 80% owned by funds managed by Blue Owl, an investment firm.
