A credit default swap is insurance on a bond. Banks pay AIG a premium, and AIG pays out if the loans behind the bond default. By the end of 2007 AIG has insured $527 billion of bonds. The contract also lets a bank demand collateral, cash held as security, when the bonds lose value. On 26 July 2007 Goldman Sachs sends AIG a bill for $1.8 billion.
