Countrywide makes $40.6 billion of nonprime loans in 2006, and $17 billion in 2007. Nonprime loans go to people with a poor credit record or no proof of income. They charge a higher rate. Those loans stay current only while prices rise. On 11 January 2008 Bank of America takes Countrywide for about $4 billion in stock.
