Lehman Brothers holds $503 billion of assets, what it owns. It has $26.20 of assets for every $1 of capital, its owners' money. It funds the rest with $133 billion of repo loans, rolled over each night. On 15 September 2008 Lehman files for bankruptcy protection. The same weekend Bank of America takes Merrill Lynch for $50 billion.
