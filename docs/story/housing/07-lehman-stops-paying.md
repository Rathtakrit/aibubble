Lehman Brothers holds $503 billion of assets, with $26.20 of assets for every $1 of capital, the money its owners put in. That leverage, holding assets with borrowed money, works only while the repo market keeps rolling the $133 billion it borrows each night against its bonds.

On 15 September 2008 Lehman files for bankruptcy protection. A bank that lived on overnight cash is gone in a weekend.

Over the same weekend Bank of America agrees to take Merrill Lynch for $50 billion, about $29 a share.
