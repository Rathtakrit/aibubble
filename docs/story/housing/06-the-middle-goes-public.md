Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac own or guarantee nearly half of US mortgages. When a borrower defaults, the loss lands on them. The two have lost about $14 billion in the past year. On 7 September 2008 the Treasury and the FHFA put both into conservatorship. That means the government takes control and replaces the management. Hank Paulson says it was the only form in which he would commit taxpayer money.
