Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac sit in the middle of the chain. They take loans from lenders and hand back bonds, and together they account for nearly half of the outstanding mortgages in the United States.

They also own loans and guarantee others, so when a borrower defaults the loss lands on them. The two have made a combined loss of about $14 billion in the past year.

On 7 September 2008 the Treasury and the FHFA, their regulator, put both into conservatorship: the government takes control and replaces the management. Hank Paulson, the Treasury Secretary, says conservatorship was the only form in which he would commit taxpayer money to them.
