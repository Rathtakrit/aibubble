The collateral calls on AIG reach $33.9 billion by 16 September 2008. AIG has posted $22.4 billion of it. Goldman Sachs alone has called $10.1 billion. AIG holds about $1 trillion of assets but not enough cash. The Federal Reserve lends AIG up to $85 billion. It takes a 79.9 percent equity interest, a share of ownership.
