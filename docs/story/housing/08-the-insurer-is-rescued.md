The collateral calls on AIG reach $33.9 billion by 16 September 2008, and AIG has posted $22.4 billion of it. Goldman Sachs alone has called $10.1 billion and holds $7.6 billion. AIG has about $1 trillion of assets, but not that much cash.

That day the Federal Reserve lends AIG up to $85 billion and takes a 79.9 percent equity interest, a share of ownership. It says a disorderly failure of AIG could add to already significant levels of financial market fragility.

The insurer that stood behind $527 billion of bonds now belongs to the government.
