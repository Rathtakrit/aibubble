Oracle and OpenAI agree a reported $300 billion of computing over about five years. Nvidia says it will invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI. AMD gives OpenAI the right to buy up to 160 million AMD shares. A right to buy shares later is called a warrant. These are promises, not money spent. The same companies sit on both sides.
