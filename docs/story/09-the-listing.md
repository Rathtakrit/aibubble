On 20 November 2026 Anthropic sells shares, small pieces of ownership, to the public for the first time. The listing raises about $93.5 billion before costs, and Anthropic receives about $91.6 billion. The price is $83 a share.

In a separate sale, seven suppliers and partners buy $26 billion of Anthropic stock at the same price, among them AMD, Broadcom, Google, Microsoft and Nvidia. The companies that supply or host Anthropic now own part of a listed company.

Anyone can buy the shares now, so the price is set in public trading.
