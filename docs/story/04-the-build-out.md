---
title: "The build-out"
date: 2025-01-21
players: [openai, softbank, oracle]
---
By June 2024 Nvidia is the most valuable listed company in the world, at $3.34 trillion.

On 21 January 2025 OpenAI, SoftBank and Oracle announce Stargate: $100 billion to start on data centres, the buildings full of chips where models run, and up to $500 billion over four years.

Six days later a Chinese lab releases DeepSeek-R1, a capable model built cheaply. Nvidia falls 17% in a day, about $600 billion of value. Then the market recovers, and the building goes on. In July Nvidia passes $4 trillion.
