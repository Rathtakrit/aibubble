On 20 November 2026 Anthropic sells shares to the public for the first time. The shares are small pieces of ownership. The listing raises about $93.5 billion before costs. Anthropic receives about $91.6 billion. The price is $83 a share. Seven suppliers and partners buy $26 billion of stock at that price. They include AMD, Broadcom, Google, Microsoft and Nvidia. This is where the story stands on 20 November 2026; it is still running.
