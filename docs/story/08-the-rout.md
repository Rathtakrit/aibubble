On 5 November 2026 the fall in share prices reaches the whole US market. More than $3.2 trillion is wiped from the value of US shares in a day, more than the yearly output of Canada. The Nasdaq falls 5.5% and the S&P 500 falls 4.4%.

The Guardian reports that investors are betting on an AI slowdown after the US midterm elections, and that some analysts say valuations had become detached from reality. On 8 October 2026, a month before, OpenAI is reported to have told investors its yearly revenue is approaching $50 billion. Earlier reports had put the figure at $70 billion.
