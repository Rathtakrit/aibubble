On 5 November 2026 the fall in share prices reaches the whole US market. More than $3.2 trillion is wiped from the value of US shares in a day. The Nasdaq, an index of mostly tech share prices, falls 5.5%. The S&P 500, an index of 500 large US companies, falls 4.4%.

The Guardian reports that investors are betting on an AI slowdown after the US midterm elections. On 8 October 2026 OpenAI is reported to have told investors its yearly revenue, money from sales before costs, is approaching $50 billion. That is $20 billion below the $70 billion earlier reports expected, not a fall. Investors care because the spending is meant to be justified by sales.
