Microsoft's capital spending, money spent on buildings and equipment, rises from $28.1 billion in the year to June 2023 to $44.5 billion a year later, and it plans $80 billion for the year to June 2025.

On 21 January 2025 OpenAI, SoftBank and Oracle announce Stargate: $100 billion to start on data centres, the buildings full of chips, and up to $500 billion over four years.

A week later a cheap Chinese model, DeepSeek-R1, is the talk of the market. It matches rivals with fewer resources and less computing power. If good models need fewer, cheaper chips, buyers may need fewer of Nvidia's. On 27 January Nvidia loses about $600 billion of market value in a day.
