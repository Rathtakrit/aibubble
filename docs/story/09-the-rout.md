On 5 November 2026 share prices fall across the US market. US shares lose more than $2.5 trillion in value in a day. The Nasdaq, an index of mostly tech share prices, falls 5.5%. The S&P 500, an index of 500 large US companies, falls 4.4%. The Guardian reports that investors are betting on an AI slowdown. The bet comes after the US midterm elections.
