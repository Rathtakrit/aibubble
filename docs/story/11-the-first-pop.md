In October Nvidia's value nears $6 trillion. On 12 November Jensen Huang resigns over reported affairs, one with a woman US officials suspect of spying for China, which China denies. The board names Colette Kress interim CEO. Nvidia's value falls to $3.7 trillion. On 23 November the board votes to reinstate him. Shares rise 32%, adding more than $1 trillion. The board's investigation is still open. This is where the story stands on 23 November 2026; it is still running.
