---
title: "The investor is also the supplier"
date: 2023-09-25
players: [amazon, anthropic]
---
In January 2023 Microsoft extends its OpenAI deal with what it calls a "multiyear, multibillion dollar" investment. OpenAI spends much of what it raises on Microsoft's cloud.

On 25 September 2023 Amazon agrees to put up to $4 billion into Anthropic, another lab. Anthropic agrees to use Amazon's cloud and chips.

This is the pattern the rest of the story runs on. The company that invests is also the company that gets paid. Follow the lines between the bubbles: many of them point both ways.
