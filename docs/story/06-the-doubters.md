Some people had called it a bubble from the start. In late 2025 they begin to bring numbers.

On 3 November 2025 the investor Michael Burry, known for betting against the housing market before 2008, discloses bets against Nvidia and Palantir. His argument is about accounting: if a chip's cost is spread over five or six years but the chip is out of date in three, profits look better than they are.

The other side has an answer too. Railways, electricity and the internet all looked like this while they were being built.
