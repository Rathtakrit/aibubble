On 28 October 2025 Microsoft puts a value on its OpenAI stake: about $135 billion for roughly 27% of the lab. That is what the share it already holds is worth after years of investing, not new money paid in. OpenAI adds $250 billion of Azure cloud to its side of the deal.

The next day Nvidia's market value passes $5 trillion. It is the first company to reach that mark.

The loop now has a price on both sides. Microsoft owns part of the lab and sells it the computing. The supplier is also a shareholder.
