In June 2017 researchers, most at Google, publish a paper called "Attention Is All You Need". It describes the transformer, a way to train a program on language using many chips at once. Almost nobody outside the field notices.

Two years later Microsoft puts $1 billion into a small lab called OpenAI and becomes its cloud, the rented computers the lab trains on.

On 30 November 2022 OpenAI opens a chat window to the public and calls it ChatGPT. This is the bubble on that day. Nvidia, which makes the chips, is a company among many.
