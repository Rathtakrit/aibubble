The companies building models like ChatGPT need the same thing: Nvidia chips, in very large numbers.

On 30 May 2023 Nvidia's market value briefly reaches $1 trillion. Market value is what all of a company's shares are worth together. It is the first chipmaker to reach that mark. The shares end the day a little below it.

The stock market's reward is not going to the labs that make the models. It is going to the company that sells them the tools.
