title: "The circle"
date: 2025-10-29
players: [openai, oracle, nvidia, amd, microsoft]
---
In ten weeks of autumn 2025 the big numbers arrive.

Oracle and OpenAI: a reported $300 billion of computing over about five years. Nvidia and OpenAI: a letter of intent, a written plan that is not yet a contract, for Nvidia to invest up to $100 billion as OpenAI installs Nvidia systems. AMD gives OpenAI the right to up to 160 million AMD shares as OpenAI buys AMD chips. Microsoft ends up with about 27% of OpenAI.

On 29 October Nvidia passes $5 trillion. These are promises, not money spent, and the same few companies sit on both sides of them.
