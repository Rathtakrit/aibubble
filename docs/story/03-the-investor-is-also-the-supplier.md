On 25 September 2023 Amazon agrees to put up to $4 billion into Anthropic, another lab. Anthropic agrees to use Amazon's cloud and chips.

A month later Google's parent Alphabet reportedly puts in up to $2 billion.

The company that invests is also the company that gets paid. Follow the lines between the bubbles: many of them point both ways.
