In 2026 the funding rounds keep growing. A valuation is the price investors put on a whole private company when they buy a piece of it.

Anthropic raises $30 billion at a $380 billion valuation in February. OpenAI raises $122 billion at $852 billion in March. On 28 May Anthropic raises $65 billion at $965 billion.

In April Anthropic commits more than $100 billion to Amazon's cloud over ten years, while Amazon commits up to $25 billion more to Anthropic. The circle from 2023 is still there. It has two more zeros.
