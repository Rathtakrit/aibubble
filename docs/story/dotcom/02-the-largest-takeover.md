WorldCom and Sprint both carry long-distance calls, and the price of a call keeps falling. On 5 October 1999 WorldCom agrees to take over Sprint for $115 billion in its own shares. Counting Sprint's debt, the money it has borrowed, the price is $129 billion, the largest takeover ever attempted.

No cash changes hands. Sprint's owners receive WorldCom shares, so the value they get depends on WorldCom's share price until the deal closes. On 13 July 2000 the two companies call the deal off after regulators in the United States and Europe block it.
