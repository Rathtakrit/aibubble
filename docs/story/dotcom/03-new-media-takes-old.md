America Online provides internet access. Time Warner owns films, cable channels and magazines. On 10 January 2000 AOL agrees to take over Time Warner with its own shares.

The Guardian puts the merger at $250 billion. It gives AOL a market value, the share price times the number of shares, of about $164 billion and Time Warner about $83 billion. Those two add to $247 billion; the Guardian rounds the headline to $250 billion.

"This is an historic moment in which new media has truly come of age," says AOL's Steve Case.
