America Online provides internet access. Time Warner owns films, cable channels and magazines. On 10 January 2000 AOL agrees to take over Time Warner. The Guardian puts the merger at $250 billion. It gives AOL a market value of about $164 billion, the share price times the number of shares. Time Warner is worth about $83 billion. AOL pays with its own shares, so the price rests on AOL's share price.
