America Online provides internet access. Time Warner owns films, cable channels and magazines. On 10 January 2000 AOL agrees to take over Time Warner.

The Guardian puts the merger at $250 billion. It gives AOL a market value, the share price times the number of shares, of about $164 billion and Time Warner about $83 billion. AOL pays with its own shares: its owners are likely to hold just over half of the new company, so the price rests on AOL's share price.

"This is an historic moment in which new media has truly come of age," says AOL's Steve Case.
