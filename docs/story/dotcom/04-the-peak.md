The Nasdaq, the index that tracks technology share prices, closes at a record 5,048.62 on 10 March 2000. Microsoft is the most valuable stock on the index that day.

The record is the peak. By 4 October 2002 the Nasdaq closes at 1,139.90, 77% below it. The index does not beat the record until 2015. The shares that paid for the deals in this story fall with the index.
