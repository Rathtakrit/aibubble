Carriers trade long leases on fibre with each other and count both sides as revenue. Qwest swaps capacity with Enron, an energy trading company that also trades fibre capacity. Qwest books $36.3 million in late 1999 and $85.5 million in the third quarter of 2001. Qwest also trades $105 million of capacity with Global Crossing in early 2001. Global Crossing counts $720 million of "cash revenue" from such swaps in the first half of 2001.
