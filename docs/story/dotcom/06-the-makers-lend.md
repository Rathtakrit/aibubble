Young carriers need gear and have little cash. The equipment makers lend them the money. Lucent promises Winstar up to $2 billion. Cisco lends ICG $180 million. Lucent opens a $1.35 billion credit line to Leap Wireless, a promise to lend up to that much. The order is booked now. The customer is meant to repay out of revenue, the money from sales before costs.
