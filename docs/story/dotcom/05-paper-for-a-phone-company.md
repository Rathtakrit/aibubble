Qwest builds a new fibre network and carries long-distance calls. On 30 June 2000 it completes its purchase of US West, a local phone company, for $48 billion in Qwest shares. It is the first full merger of a long-distance company and a local phone company, and it comes less than four months after the Nasdaq's peak.

Qwest now owns the lines into homes, the customers and US West's debt. "Distance is no longer relevant," says Representative Billy Tauzin. The upstart has paid with paper for a business that already had the wires.
