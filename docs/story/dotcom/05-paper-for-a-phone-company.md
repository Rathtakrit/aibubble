Qwest builds a new fibre network and carries long-distance calls. On 30 June 2000 it completes its purchase of US West, a local phone company. It pays $48 billion in Qwest shares. It is the first full merger of a long-distance company and a local phone company. It comes less than four months after the Nasdaq's peak. Qwest now owns the lines into homes and US West's debt.
