On 19 April 2001 Winstar files for Chapter 11. Chapter 11 is the bankruptcy process that lets a company keep running while it reworks its debts. It blames Lucent for cutting off its financing. Lucent has lent it about $700 million, and Winstar has missed about $75 million of interest payments. ICG, which borrowed $180 million from Cisco, is in bankruptcy by November 2000. Leap owes Lucent $111 million.
