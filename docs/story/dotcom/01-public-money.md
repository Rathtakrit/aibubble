Netscape makes the browser people use to reach the web. On 9 August 1995 it offers 5 million shares, parts of a company investors own, at $28. They close at $58.25, more than double, on a company with no profit. Yahoo offers 2.6 million shares at $13 in April 1996 and closes at $33. In December 1996 Alan Greenspan asks if "irrational exuberance" has lifted asset values too far. On 14 May 1997 Amazon offers 3 million shares at $18.
