# Early private valuation points — OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral

Research note, 2026-11-15. Companion to `03-values.md`, which starts each of these four
companies later: OpenAI 2024-10-02 ($157bn), Anthropic 2025-03-03 ($61.5bn), xAI 2026-02-02
($250bn), Mistral 2025–2026 only. This file collects the points that come **before** those,
so the day slider has something to show in 2019–2025 for companies that were still private.

## How to read the table

- One row per dated valuation. The date is the date the source puts on the event (announcement,
  closing, or, where marked, the date the source reported the figure).
- **Valuation** is US$ billion, post-money unless the "What" column says pre-money.
- **Amount raised** is the new money in that round, US$ billion. "—" means the source does not
  give it, or the event raised no new money (a share sale).
- **"Reported"** means the company did not announce the valuation itself; a newsroom or a source
  gave it. Every figure here that a company did not publish itself is marked reported.
- **Checked = opened** only where the linked page was opened and that page states both the date
  and the valuation. Nothing in the table comes from memory, a search snippet, or arithmetic on
  a different number, except where the "What" column says the figure is arithmetic.
- Currency: Mistral's rounds were priced in euros. The euro figures are in "What"; the US$ column
  is converted at roughly 1.17 US$ per euro, which is arithmetic, not a quoted rate.

## Not found / doubts

**OpenAI, 2019 (Microsoft's first $1bn).** No valuation was published. The Microsoft post
(`news.microsoft.com/source/2019/07/22/openai-forms-exclusive-computing-partnership-with-microsoft-to-build-new-azure-ai-supercomputing-technologies/`)
and TechCrunch's 2019-07-22 story both state the $1bn investment and no valuation. Gap: this point
cannot go on the slider as a valuation.

**OpenAI, 2021 (~$14bn).** The figure exists but I could not open a page that dates it.
Reuters (2024-10-02) says the valuation "has risen from $14 billion in 2021"; PitchBook says
"OpenAI's 2021 tender offer valued the company at $14 billion". Both are blocked to fetching
(Reuters) or are a summary (PitchBook), and neither gives a day or month. No row written. If the
owner wants a 2021 point, it needs a dated source that can be opened.

**OpenAI, 2023-01-23 ($29bn, Microsoft's $10bn).** `en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI` says the January
2023 investments "were made at a $29 billion valuation, double OpenAI's 2021 valuation". Opened,
but it is a wiki summary and no news page carrying the same sentence could be opened (WSJ, CNBC,
Bloomberg, Reuters all blocked). It is also the same $29bn as the April 2023 tender already in the
table, so it was left out rather than entered twice. Weak.

**Anthropic, 2021 Series A ($124M).** Company post (2021-05-28) and TechCrunch (2021-05-28) state
the amount and no valuation. Gap.

**Anthropic, 2022 Series B ($580M).** Company post (2022-04-29) states the amount and no
valuation. Gap — I found no page that gives a Series B valuation, only later summaries that skip it.

**Anthropic, 2023 Series C: $5bn or $4bn?** The row above uses "nearly $5 billion" from a small
private-market site quoting "sources" — the weakest source in the table. CNBC's 2023-12-21 story
(search snippet only; CNBC blocks fetching) calls the December 2023 talks "nearly 4.5 times the
startup's $4.1 billion valuation earlier this year", which reads as the same 2023 round at ~$4.1bn.
The range is therefore roughly $4bn–$5bn and the row should be treated as approximate. TechCrunch's
own 2023-05-23 story says Anthropic would not disclose the valuation.

**Anthropic, early 2024 ($18.4bn).** The number is solid (TechCrunch states it as current
post-money on 2024-05-13, and CNBC's December 2023 story had it as the price of the round then
being raised), but no opened page dates the close. The December 2023 round was reported as "in
talks"; the $18.4bn is best read as the post-money of the round that closed in the first half of
2024. The row's date is the report date, not a close date.

**xAI, 2024 Series B: pre-money vs post-money.** TechCrunch quotes an $18bn pre-money valuation
with $6bn raised. The $24bn post-money in the table is my arithmetic. Anyone comparing with other
tables will see "$18bn" and "$24bn" for the same round.

**xAI, late 2024 Series C: $45bn vs $50bn.** TechCrunch (2024-12-25) has $45bn. A ~$50bn figure is
often quoted for the same period. The row uses the number on the opened page.
