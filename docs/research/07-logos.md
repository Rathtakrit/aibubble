## Not found / doubts

- **Could not read at all:** openai.com/brand (403), oracle.com/legal/trademarks and /legal/logos (403),
  tesla.com/trademarks and /legal/additional-resources (403), spacex.com/trademark (JavaScript only),
  broadcom.com/company/logo-request and /logorequest/acceptance (serve no text), docs.broadcom.com/doc/1232742686
  (PDF, which the fetch tool refuses). Five rows therefore have an empty rules cell.
- **AMD:** newsroom.amd.com/media-center opens but is a press-asset page, not rules. amd.com/en/legal/trademarks.html
  and the media-library terms page timed out on every try, so AMD's actual logo rules are unread.
- **No dedicated brand page exists as far as I could find:** palantir (only the site terms, §9), coreweave (only a
  public style guide that lists file formats, not permissions), softbank (only a trademark notice).
- **Palantir:** palantir.net is a different company and was not used.
- **XAI Commons file is not official:** it is a CC BY-SA 4.0 upload by a Commons user, so its licence line says
  nothing about xAI. The official files sit in the x.ai zip.
- **Anthropic's Commons file** carries a public-domain tag but no trademark tag, unlike the other files here.
- **Tesla's Commons file** shows a public-domain/trademark tag pair *and* a CC BY-SA 4.0 line in its licensing section;
  both are on the page and are recorded as found.
- **Alphabet:** Alphabet's own wordmark is little used in public, so the row follows Google's brand resource centre
  and Google's current logo file (in use since 19 May 2026).
- **Older Google file:** `File:Google_2015_logo.svg` covers Sep 2015 – May 2026 and is not the current mark.
- **Intel:** the Commons title `File:Intel_logo.svg` redirects to `File:Intel_logo_(2020,_light_blue).svg`, which is the
  page recorded above.
- **Commons titles that do not exist (404):** `File:Alphabet_Inc_Logo.svg`, `File:Alphabet_Inc._logo.svg`,
  `File:AMD_logo.svg`, `File:Broadcom_logo.svg`, `File:OpenAI_logo.svg`, `File:Tesla_logo.svg`.
- **Naming change:** x.ai's brand page now presents the company as "SpaceXSI / SpaceX Super Intelligence LLC" and says
  it is separate from X. That affects how the `xai` and `spacex` rows should be read.
- **Out of scope, not done:** no files were downloaded, nothing was judged lawful or unlawful, and no page was said to
  have approved any use. Reuters, CNBC, Bloomberg, WSJ and NYT were not used because they refuse fetches.
