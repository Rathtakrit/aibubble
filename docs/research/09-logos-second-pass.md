## Not found / doubts

- **Broadcom is still unread.** The logo-request page and the "Terms and Conditions for Use" page
  (broadcom.com/logorequest/acceptance) are built in the browser: live, an archive copy of 2024, an archive copy of
  2026 and a raw archive copy all returned only the page title. The guidelines PDF at
  docs.broadcom.com/doc/1232742686 is refused by the fetch tool ("Unsupported fetched file content type:
  application/pdf"), and per the rules it was not fetched another way. So both Table 1 cells and both Table 2 cells say
  nothing or "unread" rather than guess.
- **tesla.com/trademarks has no archive copy.** The 2026, 2025 and 2024 archive lookups all returned 404, and a
  Wayback capture-list query for that URL came back empty. The Tesla row therefore comes from the company's other legal
  page, tesla.com/legal/additional-resources, which carries the "Trademarks" and "No Licenses" text in both its 2022 and
  2026 copies. That page states no colour rule and no request route.
- **SpaceX:** the current page (2 Nov 2026 copy) renders no text at all; the readable copies are 2020, 2024 and 2025.
  The wording of the "Our name and logo" paragraph is identical in all three. No colour rule appears in any of them.
- **OpenAI:** the brand page links "Marks usage terms"; the URL guessed for it
  (openai.com/policies/marks-usage-terms) returned 404 and no search result gave the real one, so only the brand page
  itself is cited. The brand page's own colour rule is about the Blossom; the wordmark text says only "DON'T stretch or
  alter wordmark in any way".
- **AMD:** the legal trademarks page recovered from the archive is an attribution list, not a usage page; the usage rules
  sit in the media-library terms and in the brand portal, both opened above. The 2026 brand guidelines PDF linked from
  the portal was not fetched (PDF). Whether the portal's sub-guides for co-marketing say anything about one-colour logos
  was not checked — they are four separate documents.
- **Oracle:** rules are split across two pages, trademarks and logos; both were opened and the logos page is the one
  cited in Table 1. The trademarks page's red-colour sentence is about partner tag lines, not about recolouring the
  Oracle logo, so it is not used to answer the colour column.
- **Table 2 is a reading of two documents at most per company** — 07-logos.md plus the pages above. Where 07 records a
  sentence about confusion rather than about permission (palantir, softbank), the cell says "not stated" rather than
  "yes". Two cells were re-checked against the live page today: mistral.ai/brand/ ("Partners and collaborators are
  welcome to use our brand"; "Do not recolor"; Black and White lockups offered) and coreweave.com/style-guide ("Use
  SVGs when possible"; Light, Dark, White and Black logo files; no permission or colour rule).
- **Out of scope, not done:** nothing was downloaded, no page was said to have approved any use, no use was judged
  lawful or unlawful, and no git command was run. Reuters, CNBC, Bloomberg, WSJ, NYT, Forbes and openai.com were not
  fetched directly, as they refuse this machine.
