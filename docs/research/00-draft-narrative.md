## 1. The paper (June 2017 to 2020)
On 12 June 2017 eight Google researchers post "Attention Is All You Need". It describes the transformer, a way to
train on language in parallel across many chips. Almost nobody outside the field notices. At the end of 2017 Nvidia
is worth about $117 billion. In July 2019 Microsoft puts $1 billion into a small lab called OpenAI and becomes its
cloud **[snippet: 2019-07-22, Microsoft's page no longer opens]**. In May 2020 the GPT-3 paper shows that making
the same design much bigger gives it new abilities. In November 2020 AlphaFold 2 predicts protein shapes, a problem
open for 50 years. The science is moving; the money has barely started.

## 2. The product (November 2022 to 2023)
On 30 November 2022 OpenAI opens ChatGPT to the public **[date not confirmed on a page that opened]**. In January
2023 Microsoft extends its deal with a "multiyear, multibillion dollar" investment; the $10 billion figure is press
reporting, not Microsoft's words. In March GPT-4 and Claude arrive on the same day. On 30 May 2023 Nvidia becomes
the first chipmaker worth $1 trillion. In September Amazon agrees to put up to $4 billion into Anthropic, tied to
Anthropic using Amazon's cloud. That is the first clear case of the pattern the rest of the story runs on: the
investor is also the supplier.

## 3. The build-out (2024 to September 2025)
Nvidia briefly touches $2 trillion in February 2024 and closes at $3.34 trillion on 18 June 2024, the most valuable listed
company in the world. On 21 January 2025 OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle and MGX announce Stargate: $100 billion to start,
up to $500 billion over four years. Six days later a Chinese lab's cheap reasoning model, DeepSeek-R1, sends Nvidia
down 17% in a day; the Guardian puts the loss at $600 billion, CNBC at $589 billion. The market recovers. CoreWeave,
which rents out Nvidia chips, lists in March. Nvidia reaches $4 trillion on 9 July 2025.

## 4. The circle (September to November 2025)
In ten weeks the big numbers arrive. Oracle and OpenAI: a reported $300 billion of computing over about five years
(10 September, reported by the WSJ; Oracle has not confirmed the figure). Nvidia and OpenAI: a letter of intent for
Nvidia to invest up to $100 billion as OpenAI installs 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems (22 September). AMD gives
OpenAI the right to up to 160 million AMD shares as OpenAI buys AMD chips (6 October). Microsoft ends up with about
27% of OpenAI, valued near $135 billion (28 October). Nvidia reaches $5 trillion on 29 October. These are
commitments and letters of intent, not money spent, and the same few companies sit on both sides of them.

## 5. The doubters get numbers (August to November 2025)
A report from MIT says 95% of company AI pilots show no measurable return **[snippet]**. On 3 November 2025 Michael
Burry discloses bets against Nvidia and Palantir and argues that spreading a chip's cost over five or six years
makes profits look better than they are. Nvidia answers in a private memo to analysts **[snippet]**. The other side
is consistent too: this is a build-out, and build-outs look circular while they are being financed.

## 6. Bigger still (January to June 2026)
The rounds keep growing: Anthropic $30 billion at $380 billion (February), OpenAI $122 billion at $852 billion
(March), Anthropic $65 billion at $965 billion (28 May). Anthropic commits more than $100 billion to Amazon's cloud
over ten years while Amazon commits up to $25 billion more to Anthropic (20 April). SpaceX absorbs xAI in February
and lists in June. Nvidia touches about $5.7 trillion in mid-May **[snippet]**.

## 7. Debt and the revenue gap (June to October 2026)
In June OpenAI's 2025 accounts leak: $13.07 billion of revenue against a $20.9 billion operating loss. In July S&P
cuts Oracle to BBB-, one step above junk, and the cost of insuring Oracle's debt reaches an 18-year high
**[both snippet]**. In September Oracle declares force majeure on a $165 billion data-centre campus **[one source,
a markets blog]**. On 8 October OpenAI's yearly revenue rate is reported near $50 billion, about $20 billion below
what investors had been told. The warning shows up in bonds before it shows up in shares.

## 8. November 2026
On 5 November, the day after the US midterm elections, the Nasdaq falls 5.5%. The Guardian counts more than
$3.2 trillion of value gone in a day, CNN $2.5 trillion. On 6 November OpenAI still closes $11 billion at a
$1.25 trillion valuation. On 11 November it reports $10 billion of quarterly revenue and a $10.9 billion operating
loss **[snippet]**. On 12 November Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia's chief executive; the finance chief, Colette
Kress, takes over for now. Nvidia's board statements of 8 and 10 November were not read, so nothing here says why.
On 14 November Nvidia is worth about $3.9 trillion, down from about $5.7 trillion in May.
