### B2. Market value with no price licence: what is available

1. **Share counts from SEC filings are public domain.** The companyconcept endpoint above returns each cover-page share count with the filing date and form type. Checked today for NVIDIA: the last value is 24.1 billion shares at 21 Aug 2026. A market value still needs a price; a share count alone is not a market value.
2. **A price quoted inside a filing or a company release** is covered by the same SEC public-information sentence when the document is an SEC filing, and by the company's own release when it is a press release. This gives values at filing dates, not daily values.
3. **An exchange or regulator publishing daily closes for free reuse was not found.** Both exchange APIs tried load by script and stated no terms in the page text I could read. The 04 file reached the same conclusion.
4. **A third-party aggregator's figures** (CompaniesMarketCap.com) may be reused for editorial purposes with attribution, per the sentence quoted above. Its own numbers are delayed and it does not list private companies.
