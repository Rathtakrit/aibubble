## What the site is
Three economic bubbles drawn the same way: one round frame packed with company bubbles sized by value, money arrows
between them, and a timeline to move through the days. Facts first: show what happened and let visitors decide.
Public, English only, no name on the site.

| Page | Path | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| AI | `/` | Explore from the ChatGPT launch (30 Nov 2022). Companies first mentioned earlier are there on day one |
| Dot-com | `/past/dotcom` | Same page, `data/past/dotcom/` (burst 10 Mar 2000) |
| Housing | `/past/housing` | Same page, `data/past/housing/` (burst 15 Sep 2008) |
| Compare | `/compare` | Two bubbles laid over each other on one scale, at start, peak or today. Leave as it is |
| Not found | `404` | Site navigation |

Story mode (chapters beside the bubble) is built but off (`STORY_ENABLED` in `ExplorePage.astro`) and works on the
AI page only. Chapters: AI in `docs/story/`, the others in `docs/story/dotcom/` and `docs/story/housing/`.

## Rules that hold
| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bubble | One outer circle as the frame. About 20 core players get a bubble; smaller ones are grouped (e.g. "Other chips") with dashed outlines. Names only, no logos: most brands forbid use without permission. |
| Size | Value: year-end market value for listed companies (plus peaks and the latest value), last funding round for private labs, from `data/values.json`. One scale per page for every day. Daily prices are parked: no free source allows showing them publicly. |
| Layout per day | Re-packed each day round the centre; positions glide, never jump. A new company enters beside the company it first deals with, and only from the date of its first approved mention. A company that was bought or failed leaves the picture. |
| Money lines | One line per pair, arrow the way money goes. Investment `#1E3A8A`, cloud `#C2410C`, chips `#0F766E`. Straight where the way is free, bending only round a bubble. Pale with nothing selected; bold in kind colours for the selected item or company. |
| Camera | Flies to the selected item's players and fits them. One focus toggle; wheel, pinch and +/− zoom 1–6×. |
| Company panel | Tap a bubble: name, value on that day with its source, its news up to that day, newest first. |
| Sources | A quiet line under the text: publisher, date, ↗, opening in a new tab. No "Source:" prefix, no raw address. |
| Look | Cream `#F5EFE3`, ink `#1B1A17`, secondary `#57534B`, rules `#CFC5B2`, accent `#1E3A8A`, key-date red `#B01E28`. Instrument Serif (headlines, dates) and Instrument Sans, self-hosted. Flat bubbles, thin ink lines. |
| Motion | Bubbles grow and fade in, the camera and timeline glide. No auto-play. With reduced motion, everything jumps. |
| Data | Plain JSON files in `data/`; every value, deal and news item carries its source link. `npm run check` validates them. News is added by hand or by a night issue, and the owner approves every item in a pull request. Science items stay in `data/news.json` but Explore shows money news only. |
| Stack and cost | Astro, TypeScript, D3. Free plans only unless the owner approves a cost. |
| Out of scope | Other languages, visitor accounts, comments, live prices, advice of any kind. |

## Timeline (restored 23 Nov, PR #79)
- **Desktop:** the Whole story strip on top; below it a zoomed line with dots spaced by real date, red dots for key
  dates and blue for smaller events, labels above and below, a "You are here" marker and a dotted line after it.
  First day (white) and Whole timeline / Zoom in (black) sit under the line. Zooming glides (about 900 ms).
- **Phone:** the same line in the sheet, without the Whole story strip. Dots are drawn but are not buttons: a tap on
  the 44 px-tall line picks the nearest item.
- **Both:** clicking before the first dot picks the first item, after the last picks the last; Earlier / Later move
  one item and keep the zoom state.
- `tests/timeline.spec.ts` checks all of this at 1280×800 and 390×844 and runs in CI.
- ~~Known bug, deferred by the owner: the timeline sometimes vanishes.~~ Fixed 23 Nov (#82): in Safari the zoomed line could shrink to 0 px wide; it now has a fixed full width and redraws when its width changes.
