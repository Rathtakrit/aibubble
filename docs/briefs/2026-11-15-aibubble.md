# Brief: AI Bubble (aibubble.rathtakrit.com)

Kickoff 2026-11-15. Design chosen the same day. Build starts with Explore and the data pipeline; Story mode switches
on once the owner's chapters are written.

## Idea
A public site that shows how the AI bubble formed. In the middle is one big circle (the frame) packed with company
bubbles, each sized by its value. Arrows between them show who pays whom. A slider runs day by day from the
"Attention Is All You Need" paper (12 June 2017) to today, with key dates marked: science milestones and money news.
Visitors read a short story in chapters, then explore any day themselves.

Built to the owner's own taste. Facts and story come first: show what happened and let people decide what to make
of it.

## Decisions (2026-11-15)
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Audience | Public. No name on the site. English only. |
| Bubble | One outer circle as a fixed frame, packed with company bubbles. About 20 core players get their own bubble; smaller ones are grouped (e.g. "Other chips"), shown with dashed outlines. |
| Size | Value. Listed companies: stock market value at each day's close. Private labs: value at their last funding round. |
| Money arrows | Investments and big announced deals (cloud, chips). Arrow points the way money goes. Colour by kind: investment deep blue `#1E3A8A`, cloud deal orange `#C2410C`, chip deal teal `#0F766E`. Amounts and sources come from approved data. |
| Entering | A company appears in the bubble only from the date of its first approved mention. |
| Logos | Each company bubble shows the company's black-and-white logo where that company's brand rules allow editorial use; otherwise the name only. Each logo's source and rule are recorded. |
| Camera | The view moves like a camera inside the round frame: it flies to the players of the current item and zooms to fit them, then moves on when the next item's players pop up. Exact in Explore and when reading a news item; loose framing is fine in Story. Manual + / − and "fit to players" buttons. |
| Structure | Story first (chapters), then free Explore. A Story / Explore switch. In Story, "Read the story" opens every chapter, past ones above and next ones below, like a ledger; tap one to jump. |
| Desktop | Direction B, "The Timeline Atlas": big date and chapter or news text beside the bubble; a two-lane timeline below (science above the line, money below) with "Before / After ChatGPT" bands. |
| Phone | Direction C, "The Market Map": the bubble fills the screen as a map that pans to the players; floating zoom buttons; a bottom sheet holds the chapter or news item, the timeline and Earlier / Later. Uses B's fonts so phone and desktop match (not yet confirmed by the owner). |
| Look | Light editorial: warm cream paper `#F5EFE3`, ink `#1B1A17`, secondary text `#57534B`, rules `#CFC5B2`, one deep blue accent `#1E3A8A` plus the two money colours. Flat bubbles with thin ink lines. Fonts Instrument Serif (headlines, dates) and Instrument Sans (everything else), both free; stored with the site, not loaded from Google. |
| Motion | Smooth: bubbles grow and fade in, the camera glides. No auto-play. Turned off for visitors whose device asks for reduced motion. |
| Wording | Plain words. Investment terms only when needed, and explained the first time (e.g. "market value: what all of a company's shares are worth"). Never says buy or sell, never predicts prices. One "not investment advice" line on the About page, not on every page. |
| News | A daily job reads news feeds, drafts items (date, headline, one-line summary, players, kind, source link) with DeepSeek, and adds them to a weekly pull request. The owner ticks or deletes items and merges; nothing AI-written goes live without that. Telegram yes/no buttons may replace the pull request later. |
| News job route | Runs in GitHub Actions on a schedule. Each run joins the owner's Tailscale network for the run (Tailscale's official GitHub Action), limited by an access rule to the LiteLLM gateway only, and calls the model through that gateway with the project's own key, so spend is tracked there. Capped at $2 a month on that key. May move from DeepSeek to OpenCode Go later. |
| Story text | The owner writes every chapter, from a fact sheet that AI prepares per chapter (dates, numbers, sources). Chapter text is copied in word for word. |
| Stack | Astro + TypeScript, D3 for the bubble layout and camera. Hosted on Cloudflare Pages (free) at aibubble.rathtakrit.com. Data lives as files in this repo, so the approval pull request is the update; no database. |
| Market data | A free source, chosen in step 3 after reading its terms. |
| Analytics | Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no banner. |
| Limits | Free plans only unless the owner approves a cost. Workers on this repo never touch the owner's other projects. No deadline. |

The design canvas (private to the owner): https://claude.ai/artifact/BAskMwaD6PktmpZSjhfi4h. Its files are copied in
`design/`: `Final.dc.html` (desktop) and `Final-phone.dc.html` (phone) are the chosen prototypes, `Bubble.dc.html`
the shared bubble with the camera logic, the rest are the three directions compared. Sizes, values and sample
chapters in them are placeholders, not data.

Design references, all award winners, checked 2026-11-15: Google AI Milestones and Search Through Time, Getty
Tracing Art (Awwwards Site of the Day); Market Map, Zoonotic Web, The Pudding's "On Upward Mobility" and "This is a
Teenager", World Bank SDG Atlas 2023, ABC News Robodebt explainer (Information is Beautiful Awards). For direction
only; nothing is copied.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Thai, accounts or logins for visitors, comments, live prices during the day.
- Telegram approvals (planned later).
- Price predictions or advice of any kind.

## Steps
Each step is one pull request a worker can finish in about 30 minutes, built on the merged step before it.
Code is written by OpenCode in supervised runs; the night shift is not set up on this repo. Research steps can go
to a helper agent. Claude reviews each pull request; the owner merges.

| # | Step | Who | How to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scaffold: Astro + TypeScript, self-hosted Instrument fonts, the colour tokens above, a build and type check that runs on every pull request, a Cloudflare Pages-ready build. | OpenCode | The check passes; the empty page shows the fonts and colours |
| 2 | Data files and checks: formats for companies (first mention, group, listed or private), daily values, deals (from, to, kind, date, amount, source) and news items (date, title, summary, players, kind, source, key date yes/no), plus a script that rejects bad files. Seeded with clearly marked sample data. | OpenCode | The script passes on the sample and fails on a broken copy |
| 3 | Market data: pick the source (question 2), then a script that builds daily values for the listed companies from 12 June 2017; private values from the hand-checked list (question 3). | Helper agent (source), OpenCode (script) | 3 companies on 3 dates match the source |
| 4 | The bubble: layout per day sized by value, positions that stay steady from day to day, grouped smaller companies, logo slots, coloured money arrows, companies entering on first mention. | OpenCode | Matches `design/Bubble.dc.html` with real data |
| 5 | The camera: zoom and pan inside the round frame, fit to an item's players, + / − / fit buttons, reduced motion. | OpenCode | Each sample item frames exactly its players |
| 6 | Explore page: day-by-day slider, two-lane key-date dots, news card, Earlier / Later; desktop layout B and phone layout C. | OpenCode | Works on the owner's phone and desktop |
| 7 | Speed: first view in about 3 seconds on phone mobile data (load data in pieces). | OpenCode | Measured on the owner's phone |
| 8 | Daily news job and weekly pull request, through the gateway, $2 a month cap. The owner first: create a Tailscale OAuth client and the access rule, add the repo secrets, set the $2 budget on the project key in the gateway. | The owner (setup), OpenCode (workflow) | A test run opens a pull request with sourced items; spend shows under the project key |

## Done means
Checked by the owner.
- **Explore, phone and desktop:** drag to any day since June 2017; the bubble shows that day's companies and values;
  each news item makes the camera fly to and fit its players.
- **Fast on the phone:** the first view loads in about 3 seconds or less on mobile data.
- **Weekly pull request:** it arrives each week; merging it updates the live site within a day.
- **Spend:** the gateway dashboard shows the job under the project's key, and it stops at $2 a month.
- **Entering:** a company appears in the bubble only from the date of its first approved item.
